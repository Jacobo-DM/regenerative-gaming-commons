This is a commons for play as a regenerative act — for attention without coercion, for small-being-safe design, for games that return us to ourselves rather than pulling us away.

You will find practices, games, and principles here. None of them require you to achieve anything.

This is not gamification. Not behavioral extraction dressed as care. Not a productivity system. Not a product.

It is offered for sharing, tending, and careful adaptation — not for capture, flattening, or easy monetization.
