🐋 Whale Song

You don’t need to know what this is.

If you like, make a sound.

Or don’t.

—

You might notice your breath.

Or the space around you.

Or someone else.

There is no right sound.

There is no wrong silence.

—

Stay as long as you like.

Leave whenever you want.

If something shifts, notice that.

If nothing shifts, that’s fine too.
