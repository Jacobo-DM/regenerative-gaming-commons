🐋 Whale Song

Begin with a sound.

This sound does not live here.

It may come from a teacher, a trusted recording, the place you are in, your own voice, or someone near you.

If using recordings, choose living sound over performance.

Seek unaltered field recordings when possible.

Avoid tracks layered with music, effects, or mood-making.

Listen.

Make a sound, if you like.

Or don’t.

There is no right sound.

There is no wrong silence.

If something shifts, notice that.

If nothing shifts, that is fine too.
